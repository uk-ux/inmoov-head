Connect the Uno. Selecting a card never moves anything.

Keys: ←/→ ±5 us (Shift ±10) · ↑/↓ prev/next channel · R rest · A / N / B record · Enter save+next · Space or Esc outputs off

Movement uses only the firmware K command, which drives one channel and forces the other 15 off. Pulses are clamped to the compiled firmware range shown on each card; the firmware rejects anything outside it, so widening a range needs a recompile. Saving stores to this browser (same store as the classic calibrator) and does not change the Uno.
